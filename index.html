How to use Wealthness

Follow these simple steps to get started.

  1. Start at the DashboardCheck your available balance, monthly income, spending, cash flow, and financial health overview.
  2. Record your money activityOpen Budget & Expenses to add income and expenses. Use categories and monthly limits to organize spending.
  3. Plan your savingsIn Savings Goals, create a target, use the calculators, and track your progress. You can also try a savings challenge.
  4. Track what you oweUse Debt Management to record balances and payments, then check due dates in the Financial Calendar.
  5. Learn and personalizeRead Financial Guides for basic money tips. Open Settings to change the theme or back up your records.
Your records are saved in this browser. This student project does not connect to real bank or e-wallet accounts. Export a backup regularly, especially before clearing browser data.

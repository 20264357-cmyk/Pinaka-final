Financial Guides

Simple lessons to help you make informed financial decisions.

🧾

Budgeting basics

Learn how to plan your income and expenses.

Read guide

Start by listing all your income sources. Write down fixed expenses such as rent and bills, then estimate flexible expenses such as food and transport. Compare your total expenses with your income and set realistic limits. Review your budget regularly.

🐷

Saving as a student

Build a savings habit even with a limited allowance.

Read guide

Choose a small amount you can save consistently. Separate savings from everyday spending, set a clear goal, and track your progress. Increase your contribution when you have extra money, but avoid setting a target that prevents you from covering essentials.

🛒

Needs vs. wants

Recognize the difference before making a purchase.

Read guide

Needs are expenses required for daily living, such as food, housing, and transportation. Wants are optional purchases that make life enjoyable. Before buying, pause and ask whether the purchase fits your budget and priorities.

🛟

Emergency funds

Prepare for unexpected expenses.

Read guide

An emergency fund is money reserved for unexpected needs. A common long-term target is three to six months of essential expenses, but it is fine to begin with a smaller starter goal. Keep it accessible and use it for genuine emergencies.

💳

Responsible borrowing

Understand the cost and responsibility of debt.

Read guide

Before borrowing, understand the interest rate, fees, payment schedule, and total repayment amount. Borrow only what you can reasonably repay. Avoid taking new debt to cover existing debt without understanding the consequences.

📅

Debt repayment strategies

Make a clear plan for paying down balances.

Read guide

Always plan for required payments first. Some people focus extra payments on the highest-interest debt to reduce total interest, while others start with the smallest balance for motivation. Choose a realistic approach and keep track of due dates.
